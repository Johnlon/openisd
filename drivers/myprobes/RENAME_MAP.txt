WinISD .wdr sample rename map

Convention
  lower-case kebab, group prefix first, WinISD key spelled as the file spells it
    baseline-      nothing entered
    field-<key>-   one key entered, sentinel 123 scaled to the key's unit
    conn-          voice-coil count and wiring
    all-           whole form filled by hand
    inconsistent-  stored value contradicts its ParState mark or its group
    qgroup-        Qts / Qms / Qes triangle
    recalc-        value cleared, WinISD recalculated the dependants
    text-          character-set and comment-encoding fixtures
