### 1. Gesture & Code Correctness ([NumInput-hooks.ts](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L432-L525))

* **Defect — Touch-hold repeat has a double initial delay (800 ms to 2nd step) and 400 ms acceleration lag**:
  In [`onStepperPointerDown`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L474-L478), `holdTimer` waits `CARET_HOLD_START_MS` (400 ms) and then calls [`beginRepeat(at, dir)`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L512-L520). `beginRepeat` sets `heldSince = performance.now()` (at `t = 400 ms`), fires only the **first** step at 400 ms, and schedules `repeatTimer` for *another* `CARET_HOLD_START_MS` (400 ms). As a result:
  * Holding for 400–799 ms and releasing produces only **1 step** (identical to a tap; the second step does not fire until **800 ms**).
  * Coarse acceleration (`caretRepeatDelayMs(performance.now() - heldSince)`) is delayed by 400 ms relative to the actual touch-start time.
  * *Fix*: Record `heldSince` at `pointerdown` (or pass it in), and when `holdTimer` fires at `CARET_HOLD_START_MS`, step once and schedule the next `tick` using `caretRepeatDelayMs(performance.now() - heldSince)` rather than waiting a second `CARET_HOLD_START_MS`.
* **In-hold finger drift cancels active repeating**:
  In [`onStepperPointerDown`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L479-L483), the `pointermove` listener stays active after `holding = true` and compares against initial `e.clientX`/`e.clientY`. If a user holds stationary for 400 ms to start repeating and their thumb then shifts by 8 px while still inside the 40×36 px button, `stopRepeat()` aborts the hold mid-repeat. Either gate the 8 px scroll-cancel check on `!holding` or only cancel after `holding` if the pointer leaves the button bounds.
* **Event cleanup, blur, and caret preservation**:
  * Splitting [`clearRepeat`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L434-L437) from [`stopRepeat`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L439-L442) and calling `clearRepeat()` in [`onBlur`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L611-L617) and [`onStepperBlur`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L506-L509) correctly prevents `focusStepper(button)` (which blurs the input or the opposite stepper button) from aborting `cancelStepperTouch` mid-gesture, while leaving the group (`!ownGroup(e.relatedTarget)`) or `window` blur / unmount still calls `stopRepeat()`.
  * Capturing `const at = caretForStep()` at `pointerdown` ([L463](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L463)) before focus changes preserves the active input's caret/digit selection.

* **Driver tab (`before-driver.png` / `prod-measured-driver.png` vs `after-driver.png`)**:
