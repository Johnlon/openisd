1. **Keyboard `Shift+Tab` re-entry ([`NumInput-hooks.ts:L101-L106`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L101-L106), [`num-input-rendition.browser.spec.ts:L252-L268`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/test/ui/num-input-rendition.browser.spec.ts#L252-L268))**:
   - After `100.0000` Hz → `Tab` → `Enter` (`0.1000000` kHz, `underlineIndex.value = null`) → `Shift+Tab`, `onFocus` runs with `entering === false` (`groupFocused.value === true`).
   - `underlineIndex.value ?? digitAtCaret(display.value, display.value.length)` synchronously captures `8` before any `selectionchange` from the browser's full-input selection `[0, 9]`.
   - `nextTick` then calls [`chooseDigit(8)`](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/hooks/NumInput-hooks.ts#L174-L182), restoring `.num-mark-pre = "0.100000"`, `.num-mark-underline = "0"`, `lastCaret = {kind: 'digit', at: 8}`, and selection `[8, 9]`, so `ArrowUp` steps to `0.1000001`.
   - The same fallback also covers reverse-tabbing into the group from outside (`▼` → `▲` → `unit` → `input`) when `underlineIndex.value` is `null`.
   - When `underlineIndex.value !== null`, `??` preserves the explicit marker index.
