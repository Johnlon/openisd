### (3) Severity-Ranked Remaining Source/Test Issues & Minimal General Invariants

1. **[HIGH] `5px` underline offset inside `20px`-tall desktop `.unit-toggle` chips collides with the bottom border ([UnitToggle.vue:L63-L64](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/ui/components/UnitToggle.vue#L63-L64), [joined-fields.css:L5](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/ui/joined-fields.css#L5))**
   - **Why**: Phone chips are `34px` tall, where `text-underline-offset: 5px` clears the bottom border by ~6px. Desktop chips are `--joined-height: 20px` (`box-sizing: border-box`) with a `1px` border (`2px` on mandatory `Fs`, `Vas`, `Sd`). A vertically centered `11px–12px` label has its alphabetic baseline at `y ≈ 13.5–14px`; adding `5px` offset + `1px` thickness puts the underline at `y ≈ 18.5–20px`, directly inside the `1px` (`y = 19..20px`) or `2px` (`y = 18..20px`) bottom border.
   - **Smallest general invariant**: In `measureFieldContent` ([fieldVisualContract.ts:L166](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/test/fixtures/fieldVisualContract.ts#L166)), for every visible `.unit-toggle`, measure text ascent/descent via canvas/Range and require `baselineY + parseFloat(css.textUnderlineOffset) + parseFloat(css.textDecorationThickness) <= box.bottom - parseFloat(css.borderBottomWidth) - 1`. (Desktop `.unit-toggle` needs `text-underline-offset: 2px` or `3px`, while `.app-root-mobile` keeps `5px`.)

2. **[HIGH] Removing `.num-input-wrap { min-width: var(--joined-box) }` leaves desktop Driver Editor subgrid inputs at `124px` even when `.unit-wide` (`--joined-chip: 60px`) is active, squeezing numeric content width to `54–56px` ([DriverEditorModal.vue:L1184](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/ui/components/DriverEditorModal.vue#L1184), [joined-fields.css:L12](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/packages/ui/src/ui/joined-fields.css#L12))**
   - **Why**: `.de-desktop .de-params`, `.de-cols`, and `.de-dimlist` hardcode track 2 as `minmax(124px, max-content)`. With `min-width: var(--joined-box)` removed and `input` set to `width: 100%; min-width: 0`, track 2 stays `124px` wide even when `.unit-wide` reserves `padding-right: 60px` (`--joined-chip: 60px`). Subtracting `6px` left padding, `60px` right chip padding, and `2–4px` borders leaves only `54–56px` for digits. Rotating `Vas` (`30.00 L` → `cu ft`, which triggers `.unit-wide`), `Cms` (`mm/N` → `m/N` `0.0004705` or `µm/N`), or multi-digit `Rms`/`Rme` risks clipping display-precision values.
