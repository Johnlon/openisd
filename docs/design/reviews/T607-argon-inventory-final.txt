### Bounded Verdict

Within the scoped inventory/test slice, the residual ordinary `Boolean` vs `BugToggle` (`bug-switch`) inventory finding is **closed symmetrically**. Both writable and readonly catalogue selectors now enforce mutual exclusion, and all four directional negative mutations are covered in browser tests.

This verdict is strictly bounded to the DOM/scenario inventory slice. As recorded in [FIELD_APPEARANCE_CATALOGUE.md](http://lap:8000/winisd/openisd-t607/docs/design/FIELD_APPEARANCE_CATALOGUE.md?html#L440-L463), no application rendering code changed in this slice, and neither the DRAFT use cases nor whole-app conformance are approved.
