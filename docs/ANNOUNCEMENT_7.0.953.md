# OpenISD 7.0.953

A month of work, in one release. Open it at https://openisd.app/ and it updates itself.

**Your projects follow you.** Sign in once with your own Google Drive and your projects, drivers
and settings sync across every device you use. Sync runs when you save, open or come back to
the app. The Cloud sync window lists what went where and from which device. Edit the same project
on two devices and you get both copies, named by the device, nothing lost. Nobody but you sees
the files: they live in your Drive.

**Your data is safe.** Every change takes a backup first. Options has one "Your data" tab: how
much space each kind of data takes, every backup with when and why it was made, restore
everything or one project, export, import, delete. Deleted projects go to a Bin that syncs too;
restore them or empty it. If the browser might clear its storage, the app tells you at startup
and on the Your data tab.

**One Projects list.** Open and saved projects sit in one panel, on desktop and phone. The open
ones are marked, the active one is bold. Sort by last edited, name, driver or size; search; act
on a row. Two projects with the same name are two rows.

**Better on a phone.** Tune and What-if are two sheets, each from its own tab on the Graph page,
with finger-sized rows and buttons and a Reset in Tune. A Save button lights up in the top row
when there are unsaved changes. The bottom bar stays put when the browser hides its address bar,
in Firefox and DuckDuckGo too. Dragging a chart axis stretches the chart, not the page.

**More boxes.** 6th-order bandpass and ABC enclosures, with rear and front chambers and tunings,
from the New Project wizard through every chart, saved and reloaded exactly.

**Charts.** The transfer function readout shows F3 and F10, and the popup jumps the cursor to
either. The active project draws thicker. The legend names each project. The 180 degree phase
wrap is a faint dotted jump, not part of the line.

**Fields that behave.** Every desktop number field has step arrows, and the stepped digit stays
marked. Letters are refused, typed or pasted. Clearing a value gives you a sensible default, and
clearing one of a linked pair (tuning and vent length, say) keeps the other as yours. Error
messages say "moving mass (Mms)", not an internal key.

**Driver library.** The list says which filters are on and has Clear filters. Favourites greys out
until you star a driver. The Model box is three times wider.

**WinISD parity.** One more WinISD bug switch: Sealed picker volume gives WinISD's box volume from
the sealed alignment picker. As always, the correct result is the default and the switch brings
WinISD's back.

**And the small things.** Dates read "16 Oct 2026" everywhere. Every export says what it exported
and where. A new version reaches an open app on its own, or waits if you have unsaved changes.
About has Terms, Privacy and a Force refresh button. Diagnostics tells the truth: "Nothing to
report" only when there is nothing.

Feedback and WinISD projects that do not match are welcome at
https://github.com/Johnlon/openisd/issues. Attach the `.wpr` and name the chart.
