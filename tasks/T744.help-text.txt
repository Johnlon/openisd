Chart from Fs, Vas, Qms: WinISD draws its charts from Fs, Vas and Qms and works Cms, Mms and Rms out from them, so a Cms, Mms or Rms you type is shown but ignored. But datasheets often state Mms, Cms and Rms that do not agree with their own Fs, Vas and Qms, and then WinISD's charts follow figures that are not the ones on your screen (E150HE-44: up to 6.8 dB apart). So OpenISD draws its charts from the boxes you see — Sd, Mms, Cms, Rms, BL, Re, Le — and marks the ones that disagree. Tick this to draw from Fs, Vas and Qms as WinISD does.
