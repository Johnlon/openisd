# BUG_20261017_baseline-conflict-blocks-landing

**Status:** FIXED (T313)

## Symptom
17 Oct 2026: two landings in one day were blocked by a rebase conflict on the visual baseline pictures and
`SOURCES.sha256`. T311 was blocked after T271 landed; both had re-taken the same 13 screens. Resolving it by
hand meant a rebase that takes main's side, which the safety check refuses.

## Cause
The baselines and their sources hash are generated by `scripts/visual/update.sh` from the sources. Two
branches that both re-take them always conflict, though nothing real conflicts. land.sh treated that like any
other conflict: abort, mark the task blocked, exit 3.

## Fix
- land.sh: when every conflicting file is a baseline picture or `SOURCES.sha256`, the rebase takes main's side
  and goes on. After the rebase, `VISUAL_RETAKE_CMD` (land.conf: `bash scripts/visual/update.sh`) re-takes the
  baselines on the rebased sources and they are committed. The `visual` and `visual-sources` gates then check
  them, and the before/after page published at landing shows the pictures that changed.
- A conflict on any other file blocks as before. A re-take that fails refuses the landing (gate
  `visual-retake`).
- Test: `scripts/land/test/land-visual-conflict.e2e.test.sh`. It fails on the old land.sh (blocked) and passes
  now.
