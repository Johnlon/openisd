Options were: lower the Vb top limit, or give the Box tab a narrower label column. FIXED in T850 (lead, QO946.c187, under John's "Clipping is a bug: fix it, don't ask"): the Vb and bandpass front-chamber Vf top limit is 10,000 L (10 m3, 10,000,000 cm3); it fits, and `layout-long-values.browser.spec.ts` no longer lists the four Box views.

Same question, phone driver editor Parameters page (16 Nov 2026, T744 merge): the Vd top limit in cm3 (100000000000) is 101 px wide and the box text area is a fraction of a pixel narrower. The cause is the ≠W cue column: main's field-layout rule 4 (packages/ui/src/style.css, "A page with a ≠W cue row reserves the cue column, which ends those boxes 10px sooner") and T744's ≠W cue on Mms and Cms in the driver editor (WinISD ignores a typed Mms or Cms). Every value box on the page ends 10 px sooner: its right edge moved from x 288 to x 278 at 390 px; its left edge (the label column) did not move, so the blue dot's room is not the cause. Options: A) lower the Vd top limit (100 m3 is far beyond any driver; same choice as the Vb limit above): the box holds the limit again and nothing else changes; B) no ≠W cue by Mms and Cms in the phone driver editor (it stays on desktop): the boxes get their 10 px back, the phone loses the cue; C) the ≠W cue sits inside the box left of the unit chip, as on the classic phone rows, so no column is reserved: the boxes get their 10 px back and keep the cue, but the phone editor's row layout changes. Vd FIXED in T744 by option A (lead, QO946.c187, under John's "Clipping is a bug: fix it, don't ask"): the Vd top limit is 10,000 cm3 (largest catalogue Vd 3.7 L), it fits, and `layout-long-values.browser.spec.ts` no longer lists `editor-parameters`. The Vb question above remains open.
