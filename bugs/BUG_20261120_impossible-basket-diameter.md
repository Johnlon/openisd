# Impossible Basket Diameter

The Driver Editor accepted 2,380 mm as a basket diameter. Old Dayton Audio DSA115-8 records could also open with 2,377.44 mm because the product page printed 93.6 inches in place of 93.6 mm. [Dayton Audio mechanical drawing](https://www.parts-express.com/pedocs/specs/295-524--dayton-audio-DSA115-8-specifications.pdf)

The editor accepts typed basket values from 10 to 1,000 mm and warns above 610 mm. Typing 2,380 mm preserves the value from before the edit, even though the intermediate 238 fits.

John's number-box ruling on 18 November 2026 was “refuse impossible, warn odd” (QO953).

On 21 November 2026 John clarified that “WinISD file's basket is read exactly as WinISD's format states it, never squashed” and that the “same applies to owdr and Owpr files”. Opening a file keeps the supplied dimensions and shows their data-quality marks. The old Dayton record displays 2,377.44 mm with a range warning.
