# Impossible Basket Diameter

The Driver Editor accepted 2,380 mm as a basket diameter. Old Dayton Audio DSA115-8 records could also open with 2,377.44 mm because the product page printed 93.6 inches in place of 93.6 mm. [Dayton Audio mechanical drawing](https://www.parts-express.com/pedocs/specs/295-524--dayton-audio-DSA115-8-specifications.pdf)

The basket now accepts 10–1,000 mm and warns above 610 mm. Typing 2380 preserves the value from before the edit, even though the intermediate 238 fits. All impossible basket values use the 10 mm minimum on load. The opening toast reports the change and the repaired field has no data-quality flag.

John's number-box ruling on 18 November 2026 was “refuse impossible, warn odd” (QO953).
